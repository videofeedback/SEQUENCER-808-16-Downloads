# Included content — v2.10.2

Both platform packages contain the same shared content:

- 16 original TR-808 factory recordings in the plugin.
- Three shared libraries: Flat Sample, TR808-VisibleFX and TR808-Original.
- 8 portable song presets, 72 pattern presets and 8 Rack module presets.
- Four complete workflow patches, screenshots, audio previews and the offline guide.

Copy Content/ into the Rack user folder, merging directories and preserving personal files. The platform installer does this for you. Shared presets and embedded-sample patches can move between Mac and Windows; choose the destination computer's audio device after loading.

Personal SP404 kits and personal expansion recordings are not included. The Working Song cover demonstrates personal sample playback, not the contents of the public download. Third-party recordings retain their own notices.

See INSTALLATION.md for paths, dependencies and playback instructions.
